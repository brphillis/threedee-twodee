A `sway` generator sends a wave down a chain of bones, for capes, banners, tails and plumes, with `amount`, `bias`, `lag`, `cycles` and `growth`. Clips take `layers`: generators composed over the clip's keys or generator at every frame, so a walk or a hand-keyed attack can keep a cape moving. The knight in `examples/characters` is rebuilt at 96 x 96 with a custom palette, rim lighting and a cape on its own bones that moves in the wind in every clip. A new `examples/fighter` project is a side-on fighting game: a martial artist whose stance, walk, punch, kick and special clips are written with the SDK using leg IK, and the energy ball the special launches, at one shared scale.
